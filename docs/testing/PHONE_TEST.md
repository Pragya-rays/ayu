Ayu phone test

One sitting of about 20 minutes covers everything. Repeat on an Android phone if you can.

**Never press Call if the 112 button opens the dialer.** No real ambulance is ever contacted.

**Setup:** on the laptop, open `/dashboard` and `/driver` at https://ayu-4801.onrender.com and press **Ctrl + Shift + R** on both. On the phone, close the Ayu tab and reopen the site.
