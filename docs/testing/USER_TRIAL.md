# User trial kit

Aim for 5 to 10 people, ideally some who are more comfortable in Hindi than English, and at least one older person. Each trial takes about 10 minutes.

## What to say first

"This is a student project called Ayu. It's a prototype, and the ambulance is pretend. Nothing real will happen, so please don't press Call if the phone offers it. You're testing the app, not being tested. Please use made-up details, and say if you'd rather stop."

Ask if they are happy for you to quote their feedback. Do not record them without asking.

## Tasks

Give the phone and say as little as possible. Watch where they get stuck, and do not help unless they are truly stuck.

1. "Imagine your father has collapsed at home. Use this app to get help."
2. "Now imagine you have a burning feeling in your stomach at night. Ask Ayu about it."

## Questions afterwards

1. What did you think the app was doing at each step?
2. Was anything confusing?
3. Did you trust it? Why or why not?
4. Was the voice clear? Did any words sound wrong?
5. Did it feel fast enough?
6. Would you use this in a real emergency, or call someone first? Why?
7. What would you change first?
8. Out of 5, how easy was it?
