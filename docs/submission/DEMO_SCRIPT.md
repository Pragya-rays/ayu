# Ayu demo video

Two cuts from one recording session: **60 seconds** for the Demo Day Drop award, and an optional **2 to 3 minute** version.

## Before you record

- Open the site 2 minutes early, because Render's free plan takes about a minute to wake.
- Refresh the dashboard with **Ctrl + Shift + R**, and the driver view too.
- Record the phone and laptop together, for example with a second phone on a stand.
- Use a little real background noise, like a fan or TV. Noise is the point of Ayu.
- **Never press Call on the 112 dialer.**
- Use only your own voice, or someone who has agreed. Do not show your real location on camera: use the area name, or zoom past the coordinates.
- If first aid is still a placeholder, show it honestly. Do not cut it to look finished.

## 60-second cut

| Time | On screen | Say or show |
|---|---|---|
| 0-6 s | You, phone in hand | "It's 2 AM. Someone collapses. You're panicking, and nobody picks up." |
| 6-12 s | Tap SOS, countdown | "One tap. Ayu shares your location and requests an ambulance before asking anything." |
| 12-25 s | Ayu asks; you answer in noisy Hinglish | Let the app speak. Show your words appearing correctly |
| 25-35 s | Dashboard, then driver view | Show the brief updating live. Click Accept. "The hospital sees this instantly." |
| 35-43 s | Phone: area read-back, ETA | Let Ayu read the area and announce the ambulance |
| 43-52 s | Normal mode: "pet mein jalan, jabde mein dard" | Show "Switch to SOS?" "It spots warning signs." |
| 52-60 s | End card | "Built on Gnani Prisma and Timbre. Dispatch is simulated. First aid must be doctor-reviewed before real use." |

## Longer cut, 2 to 3 minutes

Add these after the 60-second story:

1. **Interrupting:** talk over Ayu, and show it stop.
2. **Location correction:** say "nahi" at read-back, then give a landmark. Show "location updated" on the dashboard.
3. **No acknowledgement:** let 60 seconds pass, and show Ayu saying to call 112.
4. **Offline:** turn on airplane mode, and show the app opening and saying to call 112.
5. **Scorecard:** show the table, including failures, and say the calls were synthetic if no real ones were added.
6. **Limitations and next steps,** in 15 seconds.

## Words to avoid

Do not say "dispatches an ambulance", "saves lives", "clinically validated", or "real-time ambulance tracking". Say "simulated" whenever dispatch appears.
