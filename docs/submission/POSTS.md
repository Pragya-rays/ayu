## Post 2: something hard I learned (mid-way)

One word broke our emergency assistant.

A caller said "bol nahi pa raha" (he can't speak). Ayu heard "nahi" and marked the person as not responding, which picked the wrong script for someone choking.

The fix was treating a long sentence differently from a one-word answer. Another test caught "he stopped breathing" being saved as an address.

That's why we test every path before anyone relies on it.
